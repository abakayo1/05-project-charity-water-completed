A charity: water game

Water Drop

Catch the good drops. Help bring clean water closer.

How to play

Catch falling water drops before time runs out.

  • Use the left and right arrow keys, A and D, or drag the bucket.
  • Catch blue drops in the bucket for 1 point each. Missing one costs 1 point; Jerry cans are worth 10.
  • Avoid brown polluted water; catching it costs 5 points.
  • Drops only score when they touch the bucket, except for the blue-drop miss penalty.
